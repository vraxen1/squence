A little more personal.

Here’s the direction we’re exploring for Squence accounts and Veya ID-style unified identity ideas — simple, useful and built to grow with the ecosystem.

◈

One Squence ID

A single identity designed to connect supported Squence services without making every page feel separate.

♡

Your saved things

A place for favourites, stories and discoveries you want to find again when you need them.

⌘

Your preferences

Keep your experience feeling like yours, with personal choices and settings in one place.

▣

Connected experiences

Over time, an account could link supported features across Squence rather than keeping them isolated.

☁

Sync, thoughtfully

Cloud sync and backups are ideas for the future, with clear controls and privacy considered from the start.

⌑

Choice stays yours

The goal is to make accounts useful without forcing everyone to sign up just to explore Squence.

This page is a concept preview, not a working account service. No email address is collected here, and there is no sign-up form until account creation is actually ready.
